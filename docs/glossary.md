# 术语对照表 / Glossary

> 这份文档回答一个问题：**白皮书与正典里的某个术语，准确的意思是什么、出处在哪里？**
> This document answers one question: **what exactly does a given term in the white paper and the canon mean, and where does it come from?**

术语的**准确定义以正典为准**；本表只做对照与定位，不新增或改写定义。凡本表与正典冲突之处，以正典为准。
本表与白皮书术语表（表 7）同源，并增加六层名、三带、三层次/三维度、M/F 与假设编号。

Definitions belong to the canon. This table only maps and locates them; it neither adds nor rewrites
anything, and the canon prevails on conflict. It shares its source with white paper Table 7 and adds
the layer names, bands, levels and dimensions, the M/F identifiers, and the hypothesis identifiers.

| 中文 | English | 出处 Source |

> ### ⚠️ 两组「维度」不是同一件事 / Two different sets of "dimensions"
>
> - **三维度 = 方向 · 价值 · 边界**（正典 3.2），是一**次意义判断**要回答的三个问题，也是 L3 的操作框架。
> - **四维度 = 认知 C · 价值 V · 存在 E · 行动 A**（正典 8.2），是**光谱带上的四个耦合维度**，
>   用于系缚（BS）与脱耦分析（正典 6 章 H16、8.2）。
>
> 两者不可互换。本书中凡说「L3 三维度」均指前者；凡说「系缚强度 BS」均指后者。
>
> **Three dimensions** (direction / value / boundary, Canon §3.2) are the three questions a single
> meaning judgment answers, and they are L3's operating frame. **Four dimensions** (cognitive C /
> value V / existential E / action A, Canon §8.2) are the coupled dimensions of the spectral band,
> used for binding (BS) and decoupling analysis. They are not interchangeable.

> ### ⚠️ V7 用光谱带取代了固定层次 / V7 replaced fixed levels with a spectral band
>
> 正典第 3 章 3.1 给出的是**离散三层次**；而 V7 的**第 8 章 8.1「光谱带模型取代固定层次」**
> 明确把三层重释为**一条连续光谱带**：原元意义层 → 光谱带上端（高抽象、长时距、低频率），
> 原情境意义层 → 光谱带下端（高具体、短时距、高频率），「中间不存在断层，而是连续过渡」。
>
> 引用「三层次」时应同时标注 3.1 与第 8 章，否则会把 V7 已经放弃的离散模型当成现行表述。
>
> Canon §3.1 states **discrete** levels; V7's **§8.1 ("the spectral band model replaces fixed
> levels")** reinterprets them as **one continuous band** — the former meta-meaning level at the
> abstract end, the former situational level at the concrete end, with "no fault line in between,
> only continuous transition." Cite both §3.1 and Chapter 8, or the superseded discrete model will
> be read as current.

- **三层次**：元意义层承载终极方向；中介意义层由制度、文化、语言等载体承担；情境意义层是即时判断。
  把情境层的即时判断当作元意义层的终极方向，是正典 3.4 指出的典型结构故障。
  （V7 已按第 8 章把三者重释为连续光谱带的两端，见上框。）
  **Three levels**: meta-meaning carries ultimate direction; the mediating level is carried by institutions, culture, and language; the situational level is immediate judgment. Treating the latter as the former is the structural failure named in Canon §3.4. (V7 reinterprets them as two ends of a continuous band — see the box above.)
- **三维度**：L3 的操作方式，就是把材料按方向、价值、边界三问逐一定位；价值维度内部再分效用价值、
  内在价值与关系价值。
  **Three dimensions**: L3 locates material by asking the direction, value, and boundary questions in turn; within value, utility, intrinsic, and relational value are distinguished.
- **五尺度**：个人 / 组织 / 城市 / 国家 / 人类。正典公理三指出多尺度意义**不可通约**，协调不是加权求和；
  因此 L5 遇尺度冲突时须回到元意义层递归校准（正典 4.3）。
  **Five scales**: individual / organizational / urban / national / humanity. Canon Axiom Three holds that multi-scale meanings are **incommensurable** and coordination is not weighted summation; hence the recursive calibration in Canon §4.3.
