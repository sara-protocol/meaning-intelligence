## Tool 5 — Organizational Zeroing Protocol (Strategic)

*Canon Appendix A · A.2 Organizational-Scale Tools · Tool 5*

## 用途 / Purpose

**中文**　在组织尺度上按需执行一次**方向重置**：当现行战略的意义结构已经无法解释现实、
或使命压力测试（工具4）暴露出尺度背离时，用**五步软归零流程**把战略结论送回元问题层重算。
与个人版的关键差别：**组织可以被暂停的只有「结论」，不是「组织本身」**——归零期间业务、合同、
人员与安全责任照常运行，这一点必须在预检中写明。

**English**　At the organizational scale, this protocol runs an on-demand **direction reset**: when the
current strategy's meaning structure no longer explains reality, or when the Mission Stress Test (Tool 4)
exposes scale divergence, a **five-step soft zeroing flow** returns strategic conclusions to the
meta-question layer. The key difference from the personal form: **only conclusions can be suspended, never
the organization itself** — operations, contracts, staff, and safety duties continue throughout, and the
pre-check must say so explicitly.

---

## 适用尺度与频率 / Scale and Cadence

- **尺度 / Scale**：组织 / Organizational
- **频率 / Cadence**：**按需**触发（不设固定周期）；触发条件须在预检中写明并留痕
  / **On demand** (no fixed cycle); the trigger condition must be written down in the pre-check
- **典型触发 / Typical triggers**：工具4 报出三级偏离；跨尺度长期背离；重大外部环境变化
  / Tool 4 reports level-three deviation; sustained cross-scale divergence; major external change
- **前置动作 / Prerequisite**：全员相关方先过 [`10-zeroing-safety-checklist.md`](10-zeroing-safety-checklist.md)

## 关联六层 / Layers Served

| 层 Layer | 本协议做什么 What this protocol does here |
|---|---|
| **L5 方向选择** Direction Selection | 五尺度重算、方向声明与偏离阈值的重新预注册 |
| **L6 反思与审视递归** Recursive Reflection and Review | 主层：MCA（7B.2）· SA（7B.3）· AZ（7B.4）三特性在组织层面的部署 |

**为什么归零必须由元带主导**：只有元带能改写下层前提。若归零最终只调整了汇报格式或措辞（L1–L2 层动作），
它就没有触及前提，属于**未发生的归零**。 / **Why the meta band must lead**: only the meta band rewrites
premises. If the zeroing ends up changing reporting formats or wording (L1–L2 actions), no premise moved and
the zeroing did not happen.

## 五步软归零流程 / The Five-Step Soft Zeroing Flow

正典规定：**预检 → 悬置 → 回归元问题 → 重建 → 试运行**，每步设有安全检查点。

The canon specifies **Pre-check → Suspension → Return to the meta-question → Reconstruction → Trial run**,
each with a safety check point.

> - [ ] 组织未处于急性危机（或已明确危机中的归零另设规程） / Not in acute crisis, or a crisis-mode procedure is explicitly in place
> - [ ] 业务连续性与安全责任已有明确承担人 / Continuity and safety duties have named owners

> - [ ] 一线与受影响方各至少一人进入流程 / At least one frontline and one affected-party representative are in the process

## 判读标准 / How to read the result

**中文**
- **偏离阈值（正典给出）**：一级修正 <10%、二级修正 10–30%、三级修正 >30%。本协议**唯一**的数值判读线。
- **螺旋几何（正典给出）**：落点与上一轮同角度、更大半径；完全重合即无意义增量。
- **L6 失败阈值（正典给出）**：**RCT 无显著差异（d<0.3）**时，归零类动作**放弃有效性主张，
  仅保留为操作规程**。这一条适用于本协议。
- **正典未给出阈值**：RZ、SD、DC 的具体数值分界正典未给出；本表不设判读线，只做记录与纵向比对。
- **不可用「整体说得通」免检**；处理结果只有**标记**或**删除**两种，不含「保留但降低声量」。

**English**
- **Deviation thresholds (canon)**: level-one <10%, level-two 10–30%, level-three >30% — the **only**
  numerical reading line in this protocol.
- **Spiral geometry (canon)**: same angle, larger radius; exact coincidence means no meaning increment.
- **L6 failure threshold (canon)**: on **no significant difference in the RCT (d<0.3)**, zeroing-type
  actions **drop the efficacy claim and remain procedural only**.
- **No threshold in the canon** for RZ, SD, or DC; record and compare only.
- **"It all makes sense" is not an exemption**; the only dispositions are **marked** or **removed** — never
  "kept but discussed less".

## 失败即删的提示 / If Falsified, Revise or Withdraw

1. **H12（归零防僵化）**被证伪 ⇒ 五步流程整条删除，不得保留为「管理好习惯」。
   / **H12 falsified** ⇒ delete the entire five-step flow.
2. **H14（无归零-僵化）**被证伪 ⇒ 归零由「必要」降为「可选」，本协议不再作为常规机制。
   / **H14 falsified** ⇒ zeroing becomes optional.
3. **H13（元认知-判断质量）**被证伪 ⇒ 步骤 3 的元问题填写应移除，其价值主张正来自 H13。
   / **H13 falsified** ⇒ remove step 3.
4. **H8（尺度背离-危机）**被证伪 ⇒ 步骤 3 的五尺度表失去预测力，须重估五尺度模型。
   / **H8 falsified** ⇒ the five-scale table loses predictive force; reassess the model.
5. 若 L6 触及阈值（RCT，d<0.3），本协议按
   [`../docs/failure-matrix.md`](../docs/failure-matrix.md) 保留为规程，不再主张有效性。
   / If L6 reaches its threshold, keep as procedure without an efficacy claim.

## 边界 / Boundaries

- **不能替代专业判断**：不替代法律、财务、合规、安全与行业专业判断。
  **Does not replace professional judgment** — legal, financial, compliance, safety, or domain expertise.
- **不承诺正确答案**：归零输出「更值得的方向」，不承诺该方向长期正确，也不承诺唯一解。
  **Promises no correct answer**; the output is a more worth-going direction, not a unique or permanently correct one.
- **不适用于无主体系统**：纯物理系统不适用。
  **Not applicable to subject-less systems.**
- **文化敏感性**：五尺度中的国家与人类两端、以及「谁有权归零」都依赖文化背景。
  **Cultural sensitivity**: the national/human scales and the question of who may call a zeroing are culture-dependent.
- **过度元认知可致决策瘫痪**：组织级归零若超过预设悬置期仍无结论，应终止流程并由问责人直接决策。
  **Excessive metacognition can cause decision paralysis**: if the suspension period ends without a
  conclusion, terminate and let the accountable owner decide.
