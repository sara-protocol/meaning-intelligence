# 工具7 · 城市意义普查问卷（市民版）
## Tool 7 — Urban Meaning Survey (Citizen Form)

**出处 / Source**：正典附录 A · A.3 城市尺度工具 · 工具7
*Canon Appendix A · A.3 Urban-Scale Tools · Tool 7*

---

## 用途 / Purpose

**中文**　在城市尺度上周期性测量市民对「这座城市对我意味着什么」的感知，并把结果按**连续光谱带**
定位，而不是把回答塞进几个离散层次。问卷不判定哪一座城市「更有意义」，它只登记市民在方向、价值、
边界三问上的分布，以及这些分布随时间的移动。

**English**　At the urban scale, this survey measures periodically how citizens perceive what the city
means to them and locates the answers on the **continuous spectral band** rather than sorting them into
discrete levels. It does not rank which city "has more meaning"; it logs the distribution of answers to the
direction, value, and boundary questions, and how that distribution moves over time.

---

## 适用尺度与频率 / Scale and Cadence

- **尺度 / Scale**：城市 / Urban
- **频率 / Cadence**：每 2–5 年一次；同一城市两轮之间**题项不得改动**，否则不可比
  / Every 2–5 years; **items must not change** between rounds in the same city, or the rounds are not comparable
- **样本 / Sample**：分层抽样，覆盖不同城区与年龄段；抽样口径写在附录，并**随问卷一并公开**
  / Stratified sampling across districts and age bands; the sampling frame is appended and **published with the form**

---

## 关联六层 / Layers Served

| 层 Layer | 本问卷做什么 What this survey does here |
|---|---|
| **L1 减负** Cognitive Load Reduction | 题项控制在可填完的长度内；开放题设字数上限 |
| **L2 可体验** Experiential Rendering | 用一条光谱带标尺替代抽象等级选项，让定位可操作 |
| **L3 意义生成** Meaning Generation | 主层之一：方向／价值／边界三维度（正典 3.2）的公共版本 |
| **L4 价值评估** Value Assessment | 主层之一：让回答者自报依据强度，而不是替他们判定 |

**为什么不设 L5–L6 题项**：方向选择与归零是低频高代价的动作，问卷无法承担；城市尺度的归零由
工具8（城市意义归零工作坊）承担。 / **Why no L5–L6 items**: direction selection and zeroing are
low-frequency, high-cost acts a questionnaire cannot carry; the urban zeroing belongs to Tool 8.

## 判读标准 / How to read the result

**中文**
- **正典未给出本问卷的任何阈值。** 包括：样本量下限、光谱带位置的正常区间、三维度回答的
  一致比例、地方感条目的达标线、两轮之间的可接受变化幅度——**正典全部未给出**。
  **本问卷不设判读线，只做记录与轮次间纵向比对。**
- **唯一可用的既有标尺是证据等级判据**（正典第 6 章；逐级判据见
  [`../docs/evidence.md`](../docs/evidence.md) 第一节），它描述的是**知识状态**，不是市民回答的对错。
- **禁止的事**：不得据本问卷计算任何「城市意义指数」「幸福感得分」或城市排名；
  也不得把 D 节的依据强度换算成对回答的信任权重——正典未给出这种换算。
- 若某题项在两轮之间被改动，该题项的两轮数据**不可比**，须在报告中注明。

**English**
- **The canon gives no threshold for this survey** — not for minimum sample size, not for a normal band
  position, not for agreement shares, not for place-attachment items, not for acceptable change between
  rounds. **This survey reads no line; record and compare across rounds.**
- The **only existing scale** is the E0–E6 criterion (Canon Ch.6; criteria in
  [`../docs/evidence.md`](../docs/evidence.md) §1), which describes **knowledge state**, not whether a
  citizen's answer is right.
- **Prohibited**: deriving any "urban meaning index", wellbeing score, or city ranking; also prohibited is
  converting section D's basis strength into a trust weight on answers — the canon defines no such conversion.
- If an item changed between rounds, that item's data is **not comparable** and must be flagged in the report.

## 失败即删的提示 / If Falsified, Revise or Withdraw

1. 若 **H7（跨尺度一致性-生存）被证伪**，「一致者生存更久」不再成立，本问卷的跨尺度题目失去比较基准。
   / If **H7** is falsified, the cross-scale items lose their reference.
2. 若 **H8（尺度背离-危机）被证伪**——出现「长期背离却无危机」——城市尺度与上级尺度之间的关系题
   整组应删除。 / If **H8** is falsified, delete the scale-relation item group.
3. 若 **H2（意义结构的三个维度）被证伪**（意义判断方差被单一维度解释 >90%），B 节须重构或删除。
   / If **H2** is falsified (>90% variance on one dimension), §B must be rebuilt or removed.
4. 若 L3 触及失败阈值，本问卷仍可作为公共记录保留，但**不得再据以主张三维度结构的解释力**。
   / If L3 reaches its threshold, keep the survey as a public record but **claim no explanatory force** for
   the three-dimension structure.

## 边界 / Boundaries

- **不能替代专业判断**：不替代统计调查、社会学、城市规划与公共卫生的专业判断；问卷设计需经方法学评审。
  **Does not replace professional judgment** — survey methodology, sociology, urban planning, public health.
- **不承诺正确答案**：它登记感知分布，不判定哪种感知更正确，也不输出「市民真正想要什么」。
  **Promises no correct answer**; it logs a distribution of perceptions, not what citizens "really want".
- **不适用于无主体系统**：纯物理系统不适用。
  **Not applicable to subject-less systems.**
- **文化敏感性**：「地方感」「集体记忆」「公共事务参与」在不同文化中含义差异极大，题项须本地化预试。
  **Cultural sensitivity**: place attachment, collective memory, and civic participation vary widely; items
  need local piloting.
- **过度元认知可致决策瘫痪**：问卷若长到无人愿意填完，即已违反 L1 减负的前提，应削减题项而不是加强说明。
  **Excessive metacognition can cause decision paralysis**: if the form is too long to complete, that
  already violates L1 — cut items rather than adding instructions.
