意义智能 / Meaning Intelligence — Illustrated Explainer
Narration: en

01  意义智能 / Meaning Intelligence
[00:00:00,000–00:00:11,000]
If intelligence grows stronger, why can we still move toward a future we do not want? This short film introduces Meaning Intelligence. It does not replace capability. It asks where capability should lead.

02  能力与值得性 / Capability vs. Worth
[00:00:11,000–00:00:23,000]
We are good at asking how. Meaning Intelligence asks why, for whom, and at what scale. It studies directional judgment, not another ability ranked alongside intelligence quotients.

03  “意义”的含义 / What “Meaning” Means
[00:00:23,000–00:00:36,000]
In the book's framework, meaning is a worthwhile relation between action and a larger system. It is more than a feeling. A judgment names direction, value, and boundary, and remains open to revision.

04  意义势能 / Meaning Potential
[00:00:36,000–00:00:49,000]
Meaning potential describes the value distance between the present state and an ideal direction. It is not a happiness score or a probability of success. It is dynamic, and still a construct to test.

05  意义智能运行机制 / The MI Engine
[00:00:49,000–00:01:03,000]
The book's mechanism has three modules: perception, judgment, and action and four processes: identification, evaluation, decision, and feedback. They are phases of one spiral cycle, not isolated boxes.

06  可运行的智能体 / A Runnable Agent
[00:01:03,000–00:01:19,000]
A runnable agent puts the mechanism into context. It receives signals, interprets the situation, proposes a direction, asks for human confirmation, and monitors results. Critical choices remain traceable and vetoable. That is design, not proof of effectiveness.

07  六层工程栈 / The Six-Layer Stack
[00:01:19,000–00:01:39,000]
The six-layer stack moves from reducing load to experiencing generating meaning, assessing value, choosing direction, and recursive review. It is an E-zero engineering extension, not fixed canon, and not independently tested. Its one-to-six labels are not the canon's one-to-three labels.

08  五尺度导航 / Five-Scale Navigation
[00:01:39,000–00:01:53,000]
Meaning judgment spans five scales: individual, organization, city, nation, and civilization. Their meaning logics cannot be reduced to one another. Coordination is not a weighted average; conflict calls for recalibration from the meta-meaning level.

09  方向、价值、边界 / Direction, Value, Boundary
[00:01:53,000–00:02:06,000]
A complete directional judgment names direction, value, and boundary. Looking only at gains can hide relational costs. Looking only at now can forget long-term effects. Boundaries make choices open to scrutiny.

10  主动归零 / Active Zeroing
[00:02:06,000–00:02:20,000]
When a meaning structure fails, active zeroing is not surrender. It deliberately resets key premises. Reflection rewrites the next cycle's input so the loop does not repeat unchanged; it continues with revision.

11  意义自检 / A Meaning Self-Check
[00:02:20,000–00:02:33,000]
Begin with three questions: Is what I am doing worthwhile? For whom? Within what boundary? Write down the evidence and the uncertainty. Meaning Intelligence offers no single right answer, only a clearer way to ask.

12  迈向值得的方向 / Toward What Is Worthwhile
[00:02:33,000–00:02:44,000]
May every convergence move toward what is worthwhile. Understanding Meaning Intelligence is not forcing the world into one conclusion. It is keeping the courage to judge, revise, and stop. Thank you.
