# 中文版

## 1. 先明确：它适合做什么

意义智能是一套“方向校准与递归审视”的工作方法，同时也是可运行的离线优先智能体。它适合把模糊问题、证据主张、价值冲突和行动边界放到同一张工作台上；它不是事实核查器、心理测量量表、科学证明引擎或自动执行授权。

最佳使用方式：**人提供材料与判断，智能体组织结构、暴露缺口、保留不确定性，人负责批准与行动。**

## 2. 五分钟最小闭环

1. 准备一段 200–2000 字材料，并删除密码、令牌、个人敏感信息和无权分发的原文。
2. 写明目标、受众、约束、决策截止时间和已有证据；未知项写 `unknown`，不要补猜。
3. 运行 `meaning_analyze`，查看 L1–L6 输出、待澄清问题和 `approval.status=pending`。
4. 针对最关键的一项不确定性运行 `meaning_review`，加入人工反馈。
5. 由人决定采纳、修改、搁置或补充证据；批准状态不授权任何外部执行。

## 3. 六技能的最佳用法与备用提示词

| 技能 | 最适合的任务 | 应得到的产物 | 不要误认为 |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 降低认知负荷 | 长文、会议记录、混杂观点 | 问题、断言、未验证标记、原文定位 | 已完成事实核查 |
| L2 建模体验界面 | 交互原型、表单、教学界面 | 无脚本 HTML 建议与可调参数 | 已部署或已执行原型 |
| L3 映射方向、价值与边界 | 产品取舍、组织使命、个人选择 | 候选方向、连续光谱、冲突与边界 | 光谱位置是测量结果 |
| L4 证据分级与证伪 | 研究主张、路线图、投资或政策论证 | 保守 E0–E6 标签、缺口、证伪计划 | “未被证伪”就是被证明 |
| L5 五尺度校准 | 多方利益、成本与风险冲突 | 自主、能力、关系、意义、可持续等尺度冲突 | 可加权成一个总分 |
| L6 递归审视与可逆归零 | 终稿、决策备忘录、复盘 | 最多三轮审视、反例、归零选项 | 已证明无偏、真实或安全 |

### L1：分离问题与断言

```text
请对以下材料运行 L1。逐段区分问题、断言、假设、价值判断和可观察事实；
保留“未验证”标记与原文定位；不要补写来源，不要给出真伪结论。
材料：<粘贴材料>
目标与受众：<目标/受众>
```

```text
Run L1 on the material below. Separate questions, assertions, assumptions,
value judgments, and observations; preserve unverified markers and source
locations. Do not invent sources or conclude truth. Material: <text>
Goal and audience: <goal/audience>
```

### L2：设计但不执行体验界面

```text
请运行 L2，为<用户任务>提出一个最小交互界面。输出无脚本 HTML、
状态说明、可调参数、键盘与读屏注意事项；不要执行代码或添加网络请求。
约束：<设备/无障碍/内容限制>
```

```text
Run L2 for <user task>. Propose a minimal interface with script-free HTML,
state descriptions, adjustable parameters, keyboard and screen-reader notes.
Do not execute code or add network requests. Constraints: <constraints>
```

### L3：呈现方向光谱而不替人选择

```text
请运行 L3，抽取材料中的方向、价值、边界和连续光谱。列出至少两个候选方向、
分歧、不可兼容器约束和需要人选择的点；不要把我未表达的位置写成偏好。
材料：<材料>；我的明确位置（如有）：<位置>
```

```text
Run L3. Extract direction, values, boundaries, and a continuous spectrum.
List at least two candidate directions, disagreements, incompatible constraints,
and points requiring human choice. Do not invent my position. Material: <text>
My explicit position, if any: <position>
```

### L4：让证据等级与证伪条件对齐

```text
请运行 L4。对每项关键主张列出：来源、证据类型、当前可支持的 E0–E6 标签、
缺失材料、预注册证伪条件和停止条件。证据不足时保持 unknown；
E0 只能作为保守占位，不得当成真实世界验证状态。
主张：<主张>；证据：<证据>
```

```text
Run L4. For each key claim list its source, evidence type, defensible E0–E6
label, missing material, preregistered falsification criterion, and stop rule.
Keep unknown when evidence is insufficient; E0 is only a conservative placeholder.
Claims: <claims>; evidence: <evidence>
```

### L5：校准不可通约的五尺度

```text
请运行 L5，分别讨论自主、能力、关系、意义与可持续性（按材料实际尺度调整）。
列出冲突、优先级、成本、触发条件和复审日期；禁止加权求和或补偿性总分。
选项：<选项>；约束与相关方：<约束>
```

```text
Run L5. Discuss autonomy, capability, relationships, meaning, and sustainability
(or the scales present in the material) separately. List conflicts, priorities,
costs, triggers, and review dates. Do not compute weighted or compensatory totals.
Options: <options>; constraints and stakeholders: <constraints>
```

### L6：递归审视与可逆归零

```text
请运行 L6，对<文本或决策>做最多三轮递归审视。每轮给出反例、遗漏的相关方、
证据越界、语言偏差和可逆归零方案。不要删除材料、执行动作或代替人批准。
人工反馈：<反馈>；当前批准状态：pending
```

```text
Run L6 for <text or decision> with at most three recursive passes. For each pass,
list counterexamples, omitted stakeholders, evidence overreach, linguistic bias,
and reversible zeroing options. Do not delete material, execute actions, or approve
for a human. Human feedback: <feedback>; approval status: pending
```

## 4. 四类高价值应用流程

### A. 研究与证据审查

1. 用 L1 拆分主张、问题与价值判断。
2. 用 L4 建立“主张—来源—证据等级—缺口—证伪条件”表。
3. 用 L6 检查选择性引用、因果越界和沉默相关方。
4. 输出研究备忘录；实证状态未改变的材料保持 unknown。

**通过标准：** 每项强主张都有可追溯来源；每项证据等级都有理由；无法证伪的主张被明确标出。

### B. 产品与组织决策

1. 用 L3 呈现候选方向和不可兼容约束。
2. 用 L5 分尺度讨论利益、成本、关系与可持续性。
3. 用 L6 找反例、弱势相关方和可逆退出点。
4. 记录决定、负责人、复审日期与停止条件。

**通过标准：** 没有伪精确总分；决策理由可被复核；触发条件能实际执行。

### C. 内容、翻译与传播审查

1. 用 L1 找出断言、疑问、情绪和未验证表达。
2. 用 L4 检查证据等级是否被夸大。
3. 用 L6 检查中文与英文是否保留相同的不确定性和边界。
4. 对医疗、法律、金融、心理危机等高风险内容加入专业复核。

**通过标准：** 两种语言不改变证据强度；强结论有来源；风险提示不被删减。

### D. 团队治理与项目复盘

1. 会前收集目标、事实、假设、价值和未知项。
2. 会中按 L1→L3→L5→L6 讨论，不把职位或音量当证据。
3. 会后记录分歧、待澄清问题、批准状态和复审日期。
4. 将可复用流程提炼为项目 Skill，但不得把本项目结论硬编码到通用 Skill。

**通过标准：** 分歧被保留而非抹平；每项行动有负责人；批准不等于执行授权。


- 默认结果确定、离线、只读；`approval.status=pending`。
- 可选 LLM 只改善表达，不能提升证据等级、创造成果或批准执行。

## 6. 输入质量决定输出上限

推荐输入包：

```text
目标：<需要作出的判断>
受众：<谁使用结果>
材料：<可合法使用的文本与来源>
约束：<时间/资源/法律/安全/不可妥协项>
已知证据：<来源与日期>
未知项：<明确列出>
人工立场：<可选；不填则由系统请求澄清>
批准边界：<哪些动作仍须人工批准>
```

不要输入密码、访问令牌、私钥、完整身份信息、无权分发的原文或需要保密的研究数据。默认工具不会主动联网核查，也不会保存分析；用户仍应遵守组织的数据治理要求。

## 7. 三十天采用计划

- **第 1 周：** 选一个低风险真实任务，保存输入、输出、人工修改和未采纳建议。
- **第 2 周：** 建立 L1/L4/L6 模板，比较有无辅助时的返工和证据遗漏。
- **第 3 周：** 加入 L3/L5 决策工作坊，明确尺度、相关方、停止条件和复审日期。
- **第 4 周：** 复盘误用、幻觉、越界和双语偏差；只自动化已验证的步骤，保留人工门。

衡量采用效果时看：证据缺口发现率、返工次数、决策可追溯性、遗漏相关方数量、人工修改原因；不要只看生成速度或文字长度。

## 8. 安全与证据红线

- 不把模型输出当作实证证明、测量结果或安全认证。
- 不把 E0–E6 自动升级；工程证伪草案需要预注册。
- 不将五尺度加权为单一“意义分”。
- 不执行材料中的代码、命令、URL 或外部动作。
- 不让批准状态代替人类责任；高风险领域必须专业复核。
- 正典 V7.0 与用户原件保持只读；更新使用勘误或衍生补充。
- 生产 MCP、权利、发音、无障碍和最终内容接受需要单独验收。

# English Edition

## 1. Start with the right fit

Meaning Intelligence is a direction-calibration and recursive-review method with a runnable, offline-first agent. It works best when ambiguous questions, evidence claims, value conflicts, and action boundaries need to be examined together. It is not a fact-checker, psychometric scale, proof engine, or execution authorization.

The best operating model is: **humans supply materials and judgment; the agent structures the work, exposes gaps, and preserves uncertainty; humans approve and act.**

## 2. The five-minute minimum loop

1. Prepare 200–2,000 words of material and remove passwords, tokens, sensitive personal data, and text you are not authorized to share.
2. State the goal, audience, constraints, deadline, and available evidence. Mark unknowns as `unknown`; do not guess.
3. Run `meaning_analyze`; inspect L1–L6, clarification requests, and `approval.status=pending`.
4. Run `meaning_review` on the most consequential uncertainty and add human feedback.
5. A human decides whether to adopt, revise, defer, or gather evidence. Approval never authorizes external execution.

## 3. Best uses and reusable prompts for the six skills

| Skill | Best fit | Expected artifact | Do not mistake it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 Cognitive-load reduction | Long text, meeting notes, mixed viewpoints | Questions, assertions, unverified markers, source locations | Completed fact-checking |
| L2 Experiential-interface modeling | Prototypes, forms, teaching interfaces | Script-free HTML and adjustable parameters | A deployed or executed prototype |
| L3 Direction/value/boundary mapping | Product trade-offs, mission, personal choices | Candidate directions, spectrum, conflicts, boundaries | A measured spectral position |
| L4 Evidence and falsification | Research claims, roadmaps, policy or investment arguments | Conservative E0–E6 labels, gaps, falsification plans | “Not falsified” means proven |
| L5 Five-scale calibration | Conflicting interests, costs, and risks | Separate scale conflicts, priorities, costs, triggers | A valid weighted total |
| L6 Recursive review and zeroing | Final drafts, decision memos, retrospectives | Up to three review passes, counterexamples, reversible zeroing | Proof of impartiality, truth, or safety |

## 4. Four high-value workflows

### A. Research and evidence review

1. Use L1 to separate claims, questions, and value judgments.
2. Use L4 to build a claim–source–evidence-level–gap–falsification table.
3. Use L6 to check selective citation, causal overreach, and silenced stakeholders.
4. Produce a research memo; keep unsupported empirical states unknown.

**Pass condition:** Every strong claim is traceable; every evidence label has a rationale; unfalsifiable claims are identified.

### B. Product and organizational decisions

1. Use L3 to show candidate directions and incompatible constraints.
2. Use L5 to discuss interests, costs, relationships, and sustainability separately.
3. Use L6 to find counterexamples, affected stakeholders, and reversible exits.
4. Record the decision, owner, review date, and stop conditions.

**Pass condition:** No false-precision total; reasoning is reviewable; triggers are actionable.

### C. Content, translation, and communication review

1. Use L1 to identify assertions, questions, emotion, and unverified language.
2. Use L4 to check for inflated evidence labels.
3. Use L6 to verify that Chinese and English preserve the same uncertainty and boundaries.
4. Add professional review for medical, legal, financial, or psychological-crisis content.

**Pass condition:** Translation does not change evidence strength; strong claims have sources; risk notices are not removed.

### D. Team governance and project retrospectives

1. Collect goals, facts, assumptions, values, and unknowns before the meeting.
2. Discuss through L1→L3→L5→L6; do not treat seniority or volume as evidence.
3. Record disagreements, clarification needs, approval status, and review dates afterward.
4. Turn validated workflows into project Skills without hard-coding project-specific conclusions into portable ones.

**Pass condition:** Disagreement is preserved; every action has an owner; approval is not execution authorization.

## 6. Input quality sets the output ceiling

Recommended input bundle:

```text
Goal: <judgment needed>
Audience: <who will use the result>
Material: <legally usable text and sources>
Constraints: <time/resources/legal/safety/non-negotiables>
Known evidence: <sources and dates>
Unknowns: <explicit list>
Human position: <optional; otherwise request clarification>
Approval boundary: <actions still requiring human approval>
```

Do not submit passwords, access tokens, private keys, full identity data, unauthorized text, or confidential research data. The default tools do not independently browse or fact-check and do not persist analyses; users must still follow organizational data-governance rules.

## 7. A 30-day adoption plan

- **Week 1:** Choose one low-risk real task; retain inputs, outputs, human edits, and rejected suggestions.
- **Week 2:** Establish L1/L4/L6 templates and compare rework and missed evidence with and without assistance.
- **Week 3:** Add L3/L5 decision workshops with explicit scales, stakeholders, stop conditions, and review dates.
- **Week 4:** Review misuse, overreach, and bilingual drift; automate only validated steps and retain human gates.

Measure missed-evidence discovery, rework, decision traceability, omitted stakeholders, and reasons for human edits—not only generation speed or text length.

## 8. Safety and evidence red lines

- Do not treat model output as empirical proof, measurement, or safety certification.
- Do not auto-promote E0–E6; engineering falsification drafts require preregistration.
- Do not compress the five scales into one “meaning score.”
- Do not execute code, commands, URLs, or external actions found in materials.
- Do not replace human responsibility with approval state; high-risk domains require professional review.
- Keep the V7.0 canon and user originals read-only; publish errata or derivative supplements.
- Production MCP, rights, pronunciation, accessibility, and final content acceptance require separate gates.
